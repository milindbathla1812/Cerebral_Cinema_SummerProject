About Cerebral Cinema

Understanding how the human brain processes naturalistic movies through multimodal deep learning.

Motivation

Humans effortlessly integrate visual scenes, spoken dialogue, facial expressions, and contextual information while watching a movie. Understanding how these multimodal signals are represented inside the brain remains one of the central challenges in cognitive neuroscience.

Cerebral Cinema aims to bridge artificial intelligence and neuroscience by learning mappings between synchronized movie stimuli and whole-brain fMRI activity.

Dataset

🎬 Stimuli

Episodes from Friends Seasons 1–6.

🧠 Participants

Functional MRI recordings from four participants.

💬 Text

Dialogue transcripts aligned with every repetition time (TR).

🎥 Video

Visual representations extracted from movie clips.

BOLD Signal

Functional MRI measures neural activity indirectly through Blood Oxygen Level Dependent (BOLD) signals. When neurons become active, their oxygen consumption changes, causing local variations in blood oxygenation that can be detected using MRI scanners.

Project Objectives

✅ Predict brain activity from multimodal movie stimuli
✅ Learn shared visual and linguistic representations
✅ Build a Transformer-based multimodal decoder
✅ Develop an interactive brain visualization website
